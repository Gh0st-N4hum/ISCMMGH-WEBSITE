Built by the community, for the community

ISCMMGH began with a simple, stubborn belief: that no one in Vigan should have to choose between getting care and paying for groceries. It was founded by local doctors, teachers, and neighbors who pooled together what they had to build something the community could actually own — not a hospital run for profit, but one run *by* its members, *for* its members.

What started as a small outpatient clinic has grown into a full 20-bed hospital, but the promise hasn't changed: care that's close, affordable, and treats every patient like family — because in a cooperative, that's exactly what they are.

Our Mission

To provide affordable, quality healthcare to our members and community, governed democratically by the people it serves.

Our Vision

A community where no family delays care because of cost — supported by a hospital that grows stronger the more members it serves.

Our Values

Compassion, honesty, and hard work — shown through competent care and a hospital that never stops improving for its members.

Own a piece of your own healthcare

When you join ISCMMGH as a member, you're not just a patient — you're a part-owner with a real say in how your hospital runs.

  • Healthcare discountsReduced rates on consultations, lab work, and confinement.
  • Priority careFaster scheduling and priority access to our Health Trust Fund benefits.
  • Annual patronage dividendsA share of the cooperative's earnings, returned to members like you.

How to join

1
Attend the Pre-Membership Education Seminar (PMES)A short orientation on how the cooperative works and what membership means.
2
Purchase your share capitalYour initial contribution — this is what makes you a part-owner, not just a customer.
3
Get approved and start enjoying member benefitsOnce approved by the Board, your discounts and priority care begin.
